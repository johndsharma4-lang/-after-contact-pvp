50 YEARS AGO...
Humanity discovered a natural resource unlike any other.

We called it AETHERIUM.

It powers our technology. Sustains our people. Keeps our world alive.

But we were not alone.

Aurelians. Greys. Reptilians.

Three civilizations. Three dying worlds. One resource.

They came to negotiate. We came to survive.

The summit lasted seventeen minutes.

No agreement was reached.

At 9:47 PM, the White House ceased to exist.

No faction has ever admitted firing the first shot.

Fifty years later... diplomacy is dead.
